# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

**Product:** NurseryVerse AI
**Version:** 1.0
**Conforms to:** BRD v1.0 (`01-business-requirements-document.md`)
**Related documents:** Functional Requirements (`04-functional-requirements.md`), Non-Functional Requirements (`05-non-functional-requirements.md`)

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This SRS defines the complete behavior of the NurseryVerse AI system to a level sufficient for UX design, system architecture, and engineering implementation to proceed without further business clarification. It is the technical counterpart to the BRD.

### 1.2 Scope
NurseryVerse AI is a multi-tenant, browser-based SaaS application used by nursery organizations to manage branches, employees, plant inventory (as individual digital twins and as bulk stock), sales, customers, suppliers, and AI-driven horticultural intelligence, with reporting and audit capability. It excludes items listed as out-of-scope in BRD §7.

### 1.3 Definitions, Acronyms, Abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Digital Twin | The complete, continuously-updated digital record of a single physical plant: identity, growth timeline, health history, environmental exposure, AI predictions, and sale status |
| Plant Passport | A generated, QR-linked document/report attesting to a plant's species, provenance, and health/treatment history at a point in time |
| Org / Nursery | The top-level tenant entity a customer signs up as |
| Branch | A physical nursery location belonging to an Org |
| RBAC | Role-Based Access Control |
| AI Prediction | A structured, logged output of one of the platform's AI modules (disease detection, growth, survival, water, revenue, recommendation) |
| Tenant scoping | The mechanism by which every data access is restricted to the requesting user's Org (and, where applicable, Branch) |

### 1.4 References
BRD v1.0; MoSCoW prioritization convention (Must/Should/Could/Won't) used throughout the Functional Requirements document.

## 2. Overall Description

### 2.1 Product Perspective
NurseryVerse AI is a new, standalone system (not an extension of an existing product). It is composed of a web frontend, a REST/WebSocket backend, a relational database, background job workers, and a set of AI inference modules — detailed in the Phase 4 Enterprise System Architecture document. It integrates with external services for image/media hosting (Cloudinary), transactional email, optional SMS, and LLM inference (for the AI Assistant and AI-generated narrative explanations).

### 2.2 Product Functions (summary)
Organization, branch, and employee management with RBAC; species and plant catalog with digital twin records; growth/health/environmental/watering logging; AI disease detection, growth/survival/water prediction, revenue forecasting, recommendation engine, and conversational assistant; inventory management; sales/POS and invoicing; customer and supplier management; notifications; PDF/Excel/CSV reporting including Plant Passports; audit logging; org/branch settings. Full detail in the Functional Requirements document.

### 2.3 User Classes and Characteristics

| User class | Technical proficiency | Primary access pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Org Owner/Admin | Low–medium | Desktop, dashboard-heavy, infrequent but high-stakes actions (billing, roles, branch setup) |
| Branch Manager | Low–medium | Desktop + tablet, daily operational use |
| Horticulturist / Plant Care Staff | Low | Mobile/tablet, photo-capture-heavy, field use |
| Sales/POS Staff | Low | Tablet/desktop, high-frequency transactional use |
| System Administrator (platform-level, NurseryVerse staff) | High | Admin console, cross-tenant operational tooling (not a customer-facing role) |

Full personas with goals, pain points, and success criteria are in `03-user-personas.md`.

### 2.4 Operating Environment
Modern evergreen browsers (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox — current and previous major version) on desktop, tablet, and mobile viewport widths. Backend runs containerized on Linux; no client-side OS constraint beyond the browser.

### 2.5 Design and Implementation Constraints
Must use the technology stack fixed by the project charter: Next.js/React/TypeScript/Tailwind/shadcn-ui frontend, FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL database, Cloudinary for media, Docker/Docker Compose deployment. Must enforce tenant data isolation at both application and database layers. Must log every AI prediction for auditability (no AI output may be shown to a user without a corresponding persisted record).

### 2.6 Assumptions and Dependencies
As stated in BRD §8. Additionally: the system assumes each physical plant tracked as a digital twin is identified by a durable, printable/scannable identifier (QR code) generated by the system at creation time.

## 3. System Features

Each feature below is elaborated into individually numbered functional requirements in `04-functional-requirements.md`. This section defines the feature boundary and its primary user-facing behavior.

**3.1 Authentication & Access Control** — account creation, login, session/token management, password reset, RBAC enforcement across all other features.

**3.2 Organization & Branch Management** — create/configure nurseries and branches, branch-level settings, employee assignment.

**3.3 Employee Management** — invite/manage staff, assign roles, deactivate access.

**3.4 Species Catalog** — maintain a reusable species reference (care requirements, typical growth curve, disease susceptibility) used by every plant record.

**3.5 Plant Digital Twin** — create and manage individual plant records: identity, current status, linked species, branch/location, images, and the aggregated view of growth/health/environmental/watering history and AI predictions.

**3.6 Growth Timeline** — time-series record of a plant's measured growth (height, spread, stage) with photo evidence.

**3.7 Health & Disease Management** — log health observations, disease reports (manual or AI-detected), treatments applied, and outcomes.

**3.8 AI Predictions** — disease detection from photos, growth prediction, survival prediction, water recommendation, revenue forecast, recommendation engine outputs — each persisted and displayed with confidence and explanation.

**3.9 AI Assistant** — conversational interface, scoped to the requesting user's tenant data, able to answer questions and take read actions (and, where explicitly permitted by role, propose write actions for human confirmation).

**3.10 Environmental Readings** — log/import temperature, humidity, soil moisture, light readings tied to a branch, zone, or individual plant.

**3.11 Watering Logs & Scheduling** — record watering events, generate AI-recommended schedules, alert on overdue watering.

**3.12 Inventory Management** — bulk stock (non-individually-tracked plants and supplies) counts, thresholds, restock alerts.

**3.13 Sales & POS** — record sales transactions against inventory and/or individual plants, apply pricing, generate receipts.

**3.14 Customer Management** — customer records, purchase history, contact info, wholesale vs. retail classification.

**3.15 Invoicing** — generate, send, and track invoices (including wholesale/B2B terms), linked to sales.

**3.16 Supplier & Purchasing** — supplier records, purchase orders, incoming stock receipt.

**3.17 Notifications** — in-app, email, and optional SMS notifications for watering due, health alerts, low stock, AI predictions ready, invoice/payment events.

**3.18 Reports & Plant Passport** — generate PDF/Excel/CSV reports, including the QR-linked Plant Passport document for individual plants.

**3.19 Audit Log** — immutable record of who did what, when, across all mutating actions.

**3.20 Settings** — org profile, branch profile, billing/plan, integration configuration, notification preferences.

## 4. External Interface Requirements

**4.1 User Interfaces** — responsive web UI covering desktop (primary for owner/admin/office workflows) and tablet/mobile viewports (primary for field/floor workflows: photo capture, watering logs, POS). Full screen inventory in the UI/UX Design phase.

**4.2 Hardware Interfaces** — device camera (phone/tablet) for plant photo capture and QR code scanning; no proprietary hardware required in v1.

**4.3 Software Interfaces** — Cloudinary (media storage/transformation for plant images and generated documents), an LLM provider API (AI Assistant and AI-generated explanations), transactional email provider, optional SMS provider, PostgreSQL, Redis (cache/queue).

**4.4 Communications Interfaces** — HTTPS for all client-server traffic; WebSocket (WSS) for realtime dashboard/notification/AI-status updates.

## 6. Other Requirements

**Data ownership & export** — the customer org owns its data; the system must support a full data export (plants, records, reports) on request, relevant to contract termination and to the Enterprise-tier data-export commitment in the BRD business model.

**Regulatory** — where a customer nursery is subject to phytosanitary/plant-passport regulations for wholesale or cross-border sale, the system's Plant Passport report must capture the fields typically required for such documentation (species/cultivar, provenance, treatment history, inspection/health status at time of sale); exact jurisdiction-specific certification is a customer/regulator responsibility, not a system guarantee.
